Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard - five podium assets

Five premium retail and restaurant podium buildings with the Downtown Dubai Boulevard character: warm limestone, travertine and champagne stone, dark bronze frames, tall clear glass, recessed entrances, canopies and terraces. Each ends in a podium roof with a rear tower interface for docking against existing towers. All images are genuine Blender 4.5.15 Cycles renders. Nothing has been tested in Godot.

The businesses are fictional, and the combined streetscape is a presentation layout only: it does not represent real geographic placement or real tenants.
